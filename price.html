What that's next to

A part-time bookkeeper or assistant runs thousands of dollars a month — before you count the hiring, the training, and the chance they leave in eight months and take everything they learned with them.

Roughly: one day of a person's wages buys you the whole month of me.

That comparison isn't meant to say I replace that person. It's to give you a straight answer on the order of magnitude, because "it depends" is a useless thing to hear when you're deciding whether to bother.

What's in it

- The install, done for you, mostly remotely - The setup conversation that works out what I'd actually take off your week - Me — voice, email, phone, hands on your machine, and the knowledge base - Specialists built for your field when a job needs depth, and supervised by me - Every fix found on any other install, applied to yours - Support: business hours Monday to Friday, same-business-day response, and anything that has me fully down treated as urgent whatever the hour

What's not in it

A Claude subscription, in your name. That's the engine — the actual intelligence. You pay Anthropic for it directly, which also means the account and its settings are yours, not ours.

Most people should plan on Anthropic's Max plan. Pro works for light use, but real daily work will hit its limits. Anthropic changes those prices from time to time, so here's what they currently charge: [LINK]

That's the entire cost. Two bills. There is no third one.

What it doesn't cost

No setup fee. No per-user charge. No charge per specialist I build you — that's the mechanism, not an upsell.

And I never spend your money on anything, ever, so nothing appears on a card without you putting it there.

The honest note on usage

Usage is metered, and on a heavy day you can hit a ceiling and have to wait. That's real, it happens, and I'd rather you hear it here than discover it on a Tuesday.

It's also most of why I'm built to watch what I cost you: reading the slice of a file instead of the whole file, batching work, switching to the cheap model for routine chores, and telling you when a fresh conversation would be cheaper than continuing an expensive one. There's a live meter on screen so it's never a surprise.

The term

Month to month. Thirty days' notice, either direction, no reason required. No setup fee, no cancellation fee.

You keep the knowledge base and every script either way, which is the part that makes this an easy promise to make.

One more thing

Installs are done by one person. There are only so many going at a time, and that's a real constraint rather than a scarcity tactic.
