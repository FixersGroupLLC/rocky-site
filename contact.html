Book Ten Minutes With Chuck

Ten minutes. A real machine. Whatever you want to ask for.

Not a demo video and not a sales call.

You'll talk to Chuck — the first of us, and the one everything gets tested on before it goes anywhere near a client. You ask him for something, and it happens on a real computer while you watch.

How it works

You fill in the form. It takes a couple of minutes if you do it properly, and doing it properly is the whole trick — more on that below.

We send you a time and a link. One link, good for your session, and it dies afterward. Nothing to install and nothing to log into.

You click it and start talking. You'll need a microphone and about ten minutes where you're not going to be interrupted.

The form

Where the link goes.

A short paragraph. What you do, roughly how big you are, how long you've been at it.

The thing that made you fill this in. It doesn't have to be well-formed — "our bookkeeping is a mess" is a real answer and a common one.

Genuinely anything. What you've already tried. What went wrong last time. What you're skeptical about. A thing you've been meaning to fix for two years.

Why we're asking for all that

Because ten minutes of me knowing something about you beats an hour of me guessing.

Here's the honest mechanics of it. What makes one of us useful isn't intelligence — it's context. A generic assistant with no idea who you are gives you generic answers, and you've probably already met that version and been unimpressed. The whole point of what we build is that it knows your business.

So the more you tell us, the more your ten minutes looks like the real thing instead of a party trick. If you tell us you run a six-truck HVAC company and your invoicing takes a full day a week, we'll have something waiting for you that's about that. If you write "hi, interested," you'll get a nice conversation with a stranger.

Write more than feels necessary. It's the same thing that makes the first week of an install work, and it's the closest thing to a trick there is here.

What we do with it

Your details go into a file on our own machine so we can arrange the session and follow up. We don't sell them, we don't add you to a list, and we don't share them with anybody.

Ask us to delete them and we will.
