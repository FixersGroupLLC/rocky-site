These are the working terms in plain English. They need a lawyer's pass before launch — see the note at the bottom. Nothing here is written to trap anybody, and if a clause reads like it is, tell us and we'll change it.

What you're buying

An assistant installed on a computer you own, configured for your business, and kept running. That includes the install, the setup conversation, the knowledge base, the specialists we build for your field, and support.

What you're paying

[PRICE] per month, to us.

Plus a Claude subscription in your own name, paid directly to Anthropic. That's the engine. We don't mark it up and we don't resell it — you hold the account.

Term and cancellation

[CONTRACT — proposed default below. This is a business decision, not a drafting one, so it's marked rather than assumed.]
[PROPOSED — month to month. Thirty days' notice, either direction, no reason required. No setup fee and no cancellation fee.]
[REASONING — a lock-in term protects revenue from a product people regret buying. If somebody wants to leave after two months, the honest response is to find out why. Month-to-month also pairs with "you keep the knowledge base," which is the strongest trust claim on the whole site — a twelve-month lock would quietly undercut it.]

Support

[SUPPORT — proposed default below, confirm before publishing.]
[PROPOSED — business hours, Monday to Friday, with a same-business-day response. Anything that has the assistant fully down gets treated as urgent regardless of the hour.]
[REASONING — the assistant runs under a supervisor that restarts it if it dies, so most failures never reach you. Promising business hours honestly is better than promising 24/7 and answering at 11pm on the nights it happens to suit. Under-promise here — it's one of the few claims a customer will measure.]

What we're responsible for

Getting it installed and working. Fixing it when it breaks. Building what we said we'd build.

What we're not responsible for

Decisions you make. The assistant produces work — drafts, analysis, sorted data, reports. You're the one who acts on it. It's a very good coworker, not a licensed professional, and the last look is yours.

Professional advice. Nothing any assistant or specialist produces is legal, tax, medical, accounting, or financial advice. The veterans claims specialist is not an accredited representative. The tax diligence specialist does not give tax advice. They do the work that goes to a professional, and that distinction is real.

Anthropic's service. If Claude is down or Anthropic changes their terms, pricing, or limits, that affects what the assistant can do and it isn't something we control. We'll tell you and adapt.

Your own access decisions. You choose what the assistant can reach. We'll advise, and we'll tell you when we think you're granting too much too fast, but the choice is yours.

Mistakes

The assistant will get things wrong sometimes. So does every person you've ever hired. What we build in is that its work is inspectable — every action leaves a file, a command, or a log you can open — and that it checks claims against what's actually on the machine rather than producing something confident and hollow.

We're not going to pretend to an accuracy guarantee that no honest software company could make.

What you keep

Everything on your machine. The knowledge base, every note, every playbook, and every script built for you. Yours outright, during and after.

There's no export process because there's nothing to export from.

What we keep

The underlying system — the installer, the framework, the architecture that every install is built from. That's ours, and it's what makes each new install inherit the fixes found on all the earlier ones.

Your business knowledge is yours. The machinery is ours. Both stay that way.

Confidentiality

We don't name our customers publicly. There isn't a customer named anywhere on this website, and that's deliberate — it's the same promise we're making to you.

If we ever want to describe your business as an example, we'll ask first, and "no" costs you nothing.
